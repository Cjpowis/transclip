transclip GitHub

Every word of a YouTube video, on your clipboard.

Paste a link. Transclip fetches the captions, or listens to the video itself, then copies the whole transcript, ready for ChatGPT or your notes.

brew install cjpowis/tap/transclip

Free and open source. For Macs with Apple Silicon. Early preview, version 0.1.

Three steps, and you're done

  1. Install it

    Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space and type Terminal). No Homebrew yet? Get it first with the one line on brew.sh. It asks for your Mac password (nothing shows as you type) and ends with “Next steps” to paste, so don't skip those. Then paste the install line above. It brings yt-dlp and ffmpeg with it.

  2. Type transclip

    Press Return and it asks for a link.

  3. Paste a YouTube link

    The transcript is copied straight away and shown on screen. Press c to copy again, q to quit.

~ transclip
Paste the video link: https://youtu.be/…
✓ How bridges hold themselves up
✓ Using human-written captions (en)
✓ Copied to clipboard: 2,277 words

Where the words come from

Transclip uses the first of these that works.

  1. The video's own captionsWritten by a person. Instant, and usually the most accurate.
  2. YouTube's automatic transcriptUsed when it has punctuation. Also instant.
  3. Listening on your MacNVIDIA's speech model, running on your own computer. About 30 seconds for a 15-minute video, after a one-off 650 MB download.

Need names and numbers exactly right? transclip -t goes straight to listening on your Mac.

Accurate, and quick

Words wrong in every hundred, on a talk with human-written captions to check against:

Transclip, listening on your Mac 3.3
YouTube's automatic transcript 5.7

A 14-minute talk takes about 31 seconds on an M2 Mac mini.

It reaches your phone too

Use your Mac over SSH from a phone app such as Termius, and the transcript lands on your phone's clipboard, not the Mac's. Ready to paste wherever you are.

Nothing kept, nothing sent

  • No account, no cloud, no tracking.
  • Downloads go in a temporary folder that's deleted afterwards, even if you stop it halfway.
  • The only thing kept is the speech model, and transclip --delete-model removes it. (Plus the last error message, for reporting a problem, and a tiny note of when to try captions again if YouTube says they were asked for too often.)

Keep yt-dlp fresh

YouTube changes often, and yt-dlp, which does the downloading, keeps up within days. If a video won't download, Transclip tells you how old your yt-dlp is and the exact command to update it.

For the latest fixes, switch to yt-dlp's newest code with brew reinstall --HEAD yt-dlp.

Questions or problems? Ask a question or report a problem on GitHub. For a problem, run transclip --report and paste what it copies.
